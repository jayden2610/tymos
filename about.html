The Candle
Gamification, honestly done

Your candle is a clock you can't rewind.

Most productivity apps reward you with points, streaks, and badges — things that feel good but don't cost anything. Tymos tries something different.

Every session you complete burns the candle a little shorter. The flame flickers blue during breaks. Over days, you can watch it shrink. There's no way to top it up — that's the point.

It's a quiet, honest signal: this is how much of your time has burned. Not a reward. Not a punishment. Just a fact, rendered visible.

"The Pomodoro Technique works because it makes time concrete. Tymos makes that concrete thing visible."
— the idea behind the candle
