How the Flow Bar works

Wispr’s desktop overlay is a small floating bubble, not a toolbar. A circular center (mic at rest, waveform while dictating) is the only thing you look at. Cancel / Stop grow out of the sides only while a session is live. Status is color, motion, and shape — they never print “RECORDING”. The bar docks to the bottom, left, or right edge and reorients. Empty pixels around it click through. The hub is a white app window; the bar is a separate HUD (natively dark/transparent). Their white bar is also the thing people patch, because it flashbangs over dark desktops.

What we kept

  • Orb as the focal point — a 28px ring instead of their waveform.
  • Capsule that hugs the orb. No divider, no phase chip, no status dot.
  • Visual phase: gold ring + warm glass for focus, teal for break.
  • Hidden when idle. No controls on the pill (web Tymos still owns start/pause).
  • Bottom-center placement (already approved).

What we did better

  • Dark warm glass so it sits on any wallpaper without a white flash. Tymos gold still reads as the brand.
  • The ring is remaining time — it burns down like the candle, which a waveform can’t say.
  • Time is the waveform. Five tabular characters, then a quiet title. Glanceable from across the desk.
  • Collapses to orb + time when no task is focused — closer to Flow’s resting bubble.
  • Tighter chrome (~42px tall vs the old 88px window / cream badge).
