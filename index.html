How it works
🕯
Pick a task, set a timer
Choose what you're working on and adjust the session length right on the home screen. The timer counts down. You focus.
🕯
The candle burns with you
As the timer counts down, the candle burns — wax drips, the flame flickers. When the session ends, you've earned that candle. It resets fresh for the next one.
☕
Take the break
When the chime rings, step away. The flame flickers blue during your break — a small visual breath before the next session.
📈
Stats that mean something
Sessions, focus time, tasks done — tracked daily. No leaderboards, no social features. Just a quiet record of how you spent the day.
The Candle
"Each session is one candle. You burn it completely — then earn it. That's the record."
— the idea behind Tymos

Most productivity apps reward you with points and streaks — things that feel good but don't cost anything. Tymos tries something different.

Every session, you light a fresh candle. It burns down as you focus. When it's done, you've earned it — added to your collection, permanently. The candle resets. You do it again.
